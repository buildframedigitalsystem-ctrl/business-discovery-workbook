AI is considered only after the operating environment is understood.

Score each factor from 1 (low) to 5 (high). Priority considers impact, repetition, time saving, data readiness, implementation effort and human-risk requirements.

Discovery Interview Question Bank

Questions designed to uncover the real operating environment.

What currently prevents those outcomes?

Customers

How does a stranger become a paying customer?

Where do customers experience delays or frustration?

What questions does your team answer repeatedly?

Processes

Which tasks happen every day or every week?

Which activities involve copying information between systems?

Where does work usually wait for someone?

People

What work consumes skilled employees' time unnecessarily?

Where is human judgment essential?

Who must approve changes to existing workflows?

Technology & Data

Which systems are business-critical today?

Where is important information stored?

Which systems do not communicate with each other?
